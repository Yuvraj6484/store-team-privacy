Privacy Policy

Store Team Attendance (Quick Mart Time Clock)

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Store Team Attendance (also referred to as Quick Mart Time Clock, the “App”) processes information for employee timekeeping and store attendance management. The App is operated by the app developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Information we process

  • Employee identity: employee names, employee numbers or user IDs, and store assignments.
  • Attendance: clock-in and clock-out timestamps, shift history, and calculated work hours.
  • Account information: owner or administrator email addresses and authentication data used for access control; employee PINs are used for authentication and should not be displayed in plaintext.
  • Precise location: GPS latitude, longitude, and accuracy information when an employee attempts to clock in or out, to check whether they are within the permitted area of their assigned store.
  • Technical and security information: limited operational information that may be processed by our hosting, authentication, and infrastructure providers to operate and protect the service.

Why we use this information

We use these data for app functionality: identifying employees and authorized owners, authenticating clock actions, enforcing the store’s approximately 50-meter clock-in/out location restriction, maintaining attendance records, showing work-hour summaries, and securing the App. We do not use this information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking, and we do not sell employee personal information.

Location permissions

The App requests location access when needed to check a clock-in or clock-out attempt. Location is sent to our backend for store-area verification. The App is designed for location verification at the time of a clock action, not continuous background location tracking. Exact technical retention or logging of location requests may depend on backend and infrastructure configuration.

Where information is processed

We use Supabase for backend database, authentication, and application functions. Data may be processed by Supabase and its underlying hosting or infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the App. Access to employee records is intended to be limited to authorized administrators and necessary service operations.

Data retention

We retain employee identity, assignment, and attendance records for legitimate operational, payroll-related, recordkeeping, and legal purposes, only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes. Retention periods may vary depending on legal requirements and business needs. Technical records may be subject to separate provider retention periods. Employees and administrators can contact us to ask about the retention of particular information.

Security

We use authentication, access controls, and secure network connections to protect information. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

Employees may contact their store manager or the privacy contact below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, as applicable. Some attendance records may need to be retained for business or legal compliance. Location permissions can be controlled in device settings, although denying location permission may prevent clock-in and clock-out.

Children

The App is intended for authorized workplace use, not for children or general consumer social activity.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App or its data practices change. The most recent version and effective date will appear on this page.
